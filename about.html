Our Story

Why We Created Webkar Studio

Every day across Pune, Mumbai, Nashik, Kolhapur, and Aurangabad, thousands of skilled business owners do incredible work — fixing pest infestations, styling brides, tutoring future engineers, crafting bespoke modular kitchens, and curing patients.

Yet, when someone searches for their services on Google, these hard-working local owners are invisible. Big IT companies quote them ₹35,000 to ₹70,000 with confusing technical jargon, while cheap ₹2,999 template sellers vanish as soon as the money is paid.

Webkar Studio (an initiative by ebookcharm Web Services) was built to bridge this exact gap. We provide clean, fast, beautiful static websites starting at just ₹8,000, with straightforward personal communication in Marathi, Hindi, and English.
