What to Ask a Web Designer Before Hiring in India

Don't get locked out of your domain or hit with hidden annual hosting bills. Use our handy checklist of questions before making any payments.

Domain Ownership, Page Speed, and Source Code Rights

The most critical questions include: "Who owns the .in / .com domain account?", "Will you deliver the complete raw source code?", "Are there mandatory monthly hosting retainers?", and "Does the site load in under 2 seconds on a 4G mobile connection?". Asking these upfront guarantees a clean, transparent business relationship.
